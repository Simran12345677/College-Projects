## Overview
Traditional speed breakers are inefficient—they slow down all vehicles, causing unnecessary fuel waste and wear, even for those adhering to speed limits. This project introduces a "Smart" alternative that responds only to speed violations, developed alongside teammate Ajay Kumar.

## How It Works
*   **Real-time Detection:** IR sensors measure vehicle speed.
*   **Adaptive Response:**
    *   🟢 **Green / Flat:** Road remains flat for vehicles under the limit.
    *   🟡 **Yellow / Slight Angle:** Visual warning for slight overspeeding.
    *   🔴 **Red / Steep Angle:** Servo-mechanism raises the road to an inverted "V" shape, compelling high-speed vehicles to slow down.
*   **Pedestrian Safety (Smart Zebra Crossing):** An ultrasonic sensor detects pedestrians and lights up a synchronized LED path to highlight the pedestrian's path, enhancing driver awareness.

## Future Scope
Integration of RFID technology to allow emergency vehicles (e.g., ambulances and police) to bypass speed breakers, ensuring they maintain critical response times.
